# Where the animal art came from

`../animals.py` builds the barnyard's animal sheets from these. Everything here is used under CC-BY 3.0,
and `public/stackacres-td/animals/CREDITS.md` names the artists where players can read it.

- `farm/`: [LPC style farm animals](https://opengameart.org/node/11629) by Daniel Eddeland (daneeklu),
  CC-BY 3.0 or GPL 2.0. The walk and eat sheets for the hen, sheep, cow and pig. He asks to be credited
  with a link back to that page.
- `pigs-rework/`: [Pigs Rework](https://opengameart.org/node/83210) 1.1 by Daniel Eddeland and Jordan
  Irwin (AntumDeluge), CC-BY 3.0. Only the piglet is used. Its README is kept beside it.
- `horse/`: [LPC Horse Extended](https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-horse-extended) by Benjamin K. Smith
  (BenCreating), extending [[LPC] Horses](https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-horses) by bluecarrot16,
  CC-BY 3.0 (also OGA-BY 3.0, CC-BY-SA 3.0, GPL 2.0 and GPL 3.0). The brown, black, grey and gold coats'
  walk, idle and gallop sheets, the white mane, face markings and socks. Its read-me is kept beside it.
  The files were renamed (`Horse Walk Brown.png` to `horse-walk-brown.png` and so on) and recompressed,
  pixel for pixel the same.
