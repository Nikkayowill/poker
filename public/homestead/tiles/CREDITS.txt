Homestead art
=============

The Homestead's map is now drawn procedurally: every sprite (scenery, crops,
animals, buildings, and the toolbelt/HUD/barn icons) is a Canvas2D painter in
components/arcade/homestead/homestead-art.ts, baked into a texture at boot
and rendered at the browser's own device pixel ratio. Nothing here is
downloaded, and nothing on the map is a pixel-art tile any more.

The PNGs still in this folder were sliced from the Kenney Tiny Farm asset
pack (https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-farm) by Kenney, released under CC0 1.0
Universal -- public domain, no attribution required, credited here anyway.
The sheet they came from, ../tilemap_packed.png, is gone along with the rest
of the map's tiles. This folder is kept only because another branch's lobby
card still reads cattle.png from it directly -- check that branch before
deleting anything else here.

Deliberately NOT used as a source for the vector art either:
jeremyckahn/farmhand. Its art is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, and the NonCommercial term
rules it out for StackChips.
